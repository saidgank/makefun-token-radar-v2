The value model

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × maturity factor

The category multiple is measured on this snapshot: the median market-cap / TVL of the snapshot's own tokenised protocols in the category, shrunk toward the global median by sample size. A category needs at least three tokenised peers to earn a multiple of its own — below that, one anecdote would rule (the snapshot's lone prediction-market token trades near 400× its protocol's TVL), so the global median stands in. The growth factor answers the 7-day TVL change (clamped −50% to +150%, weight 0.5). The maturity factor answers how long the protocol has run with no token (0.55 at birth, nearing 1.00 after about four years). No name is special-cased: one function scores every row of the board and every launch in the blind test beside it.

The blind test model vs history

Each past launch is scored with only what was knowable the day before its token first traded: TVL that day, the 7-day TVL change into it, and the protocol's age, all from DefiLlama's public history. The headline outcome is the token's market cap on the fetch date (CoinGecko, 21 Nov 2026) — the one outcome that is fully observed. The day-30 column estimates the launch-window market cap (day-30 price × today's circulating supply): an upper bound, since supply generally grows after a launch; against it the same scores read . Two honest caveats: the multiples are calibrated on protocols still trading at the snapshot, a survivor set; and most of these tokens launched into the 2024–26 airdrop seasons and bled afterwards, which the median ratio shows.
